*The video game market size in 2026 sets a record on paper. Underneath, it is a market of higher prices, a few giant releases and players who keep returning to the games they already own.*

The video game market size in 2026 is about **$213.9 billion**, according to Newzoo's annual forecast, up 6.1% on 2025 ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/no-single-model-for-growth-says-newzoo/)). Mobile games bring in $121.1 billion of that, console $46.9 billion and PC $45.9 billion.

The growth is real, but it is not evenly spread. In the United States, where Circana tracks spending month by month, consumer spending on games, hardware and accessories in the first ten months of 2026 was 4% lower than a year earlier. A large part of the global growth comes from higher prices and from one game, Grand Theft Auto VI, which launched on November 19.

For a small studio, the total matters less than the shape: where players are joining, what they already spend their time on, and what it now costs to reach them.

## How big is the video game market in 2026?

Newzoo forecasts **$213.9 billion** in global game revenue for 2026, a 6.1% increase year on year. It counts 3.7 billion players (up 4.4%) and 1.65 billion people who spend money on games (up 4.7%), with an average of $129.6 per spender ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/no-single-model-for-growth-says-newzoo/)).

| Segment | 2026 revenue (Newzoo) | Change on 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | $121.1 billion | up 6.8% |
| Console | $46.9 billion | up 5.1% |
| PC | $45.9 billion | up 5.3% |
| **Total** | **$213.9 billion** | **up 6.1%** |

Newzoo ties much of the console growth to multi-game subscriptions, higher prices for games and services, and the November launch of Grand Theft Auto VI ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113276/gta-6-to-help-games-industry-make-dollars214-billion-in-2026-rockstars-sequel-expected-to-boost-full-game-sales-by-nearly-18-percent/index.html)).

## Where are the players, by region and platform?

Asia-Pacific is the largest region, at **$100.7 billion** of 2026 revenue, and China and the United States together account for 52% of all spending ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113276/gta-6-to-help-games-industry-make-dollars214-billion-in-2026-rockstars-sequel-expected-to-boost-full-game-sales-by-nearly-18-percent/index.html)). The new players are mostly elsewhere: Newzoo expects 117.8 million net new mobile players in 2026, and 80% of them to come from Central and Southern Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East and Africa ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/no-single-model-for-growth-says-newzoo/)). The Middle East and Africa are the fastest-growing regions for both revenue and players ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-players-could-reach-4-billion-by-2029-newzoo/)).

The second thing to know is where players spend their time. In Newzoo's PC and console report, **54% of 2025 playtime went to games more than six years old**, 33% to games one to five years old, and only 13% to new releases. On PC, the share of playtime outside the top 20 games grew from 33% in 2023 to 42% in 2025, and Newzoo expects PC to pass console in revenue within two years ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/?p=28834)).

Mobile is still the largest segment, but it is getting harder to grow in: mobile downloads fell 25% year on year in the first half of 2026, while the average cost to acquire an install rose 30% to $0.56 ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/no-single-model-for-growth-says-newzoo/)).

## Is the game industry growing or shrinking in 2026?

Both, depending on where you measure. Global revenue grows in Newzoo's forecast, but US spending is down: Circana puts October 2026 at **$4.245 billion, 12% below October 2025**, with spending on game content down 14% and mobile down 16%. From January to October, US spending totaled $45.28 billion, down 4% ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/call-of-duty-topped-october-sales-but-didnt-make-2026s-top-sellers-circana/)).

Hardware tells the price story. Sony raised the US price of the standard PS5 from $549.99 to $649.99 on April 2, citing rising cost pressures in global supply chains while memory chips were getting more expensive because of demand from AI data centers ([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/2026/03/27/sony-to-hike-playstation-5-prices-again-as-memory-chip-costs-surge/)). Nintendo raised the Switch 2 from $449.99 to $499.99 on September 1, citing "changes in market conditions" ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/nintendo-just-announced-a-price-hike-for-the-switch-2-in-the-us/)). In October, Circana measured the PS5's average selling price 13% higher than a year earlier and the Xbox Series' 29% higher, while US hardware dollar sales for the year to date were down 8%.

In other words, a good part of 2026's growth is the same players paying more, not more players.

## How much does Grand Theft Auto VI change the 2026 numbers?

A lot, for console. Newzoo expected GTA VI to drive a **17.5% increase in console full-game spending**, the strongest growth of any business model in its forecast ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-players-could-reach-4-billion-by-2029-newzoo/)). On November 20, Take-Two reported an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide day-one retail sales through the end of November 19, and 32.5 million units sold before release ([Take-Two press release, SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026075217/exhibit9918k.htm)).

Our reading: a launch this size pulls money and attention into a few weeks. For everyone else, late 2026 was a window to avoid, and the strong console total says little about how a smaller game will sell.

## What the market numbers mean for a small studio

These are our conclusions, not Newzoo's or Circana's:

1. **Players are being added where spending per player is lower.** Localization, regional pricing and modest hardware requirements are how a small game reaches them.
2. **Buying installs got more expensive.** With mobile cost per install up 30%, paid acquisition is harder for a team without a marketing budget. A direct relationship with players is worth more: by the first quarter of 2026, four of the five publishers Newzoo tracked earned at least 39% of revenue directly from consumers ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/no-single-model-for-growth-says-newzoo/)).
3. **Your competition is mostly old games.** With more than half of PC and console playtime on titles over six years old, a new game has to earn a place in a routine, and keep it with updates.
4. **PC is the friendliest platform for the long tail.** It is where playtime outside the top 20 is growing fastest. The catch is volume, which we cover in [how many games release on Steam](/blog/how-many-games-release-on-steam/).
5. **Expensive hardware favors devices people already own.** When consoles and handhelds get dearer, a game that runs well on a mid-range phone or in a browser reaches more people. We wrote about that route in [web games and payments outside the app store](/blog/web-games-payments-outside-app-store/).

## The short version

The video game market is worth about $213.9 billion in 2026 by Newzoo's forecast, with mobile at $121.1 billion, console at $46.9 billion and PC at $45.9 billion. The total is growing, but much of the growth is price and one giant console launch, while US spending is down 4% for the year to October. For small studios, the useful signals are where new players come from, how much of their time old games keep, and how expensive buying installs has become. Plan for modest hardware, many languages and a long life, and treat a direct line to players as an asset.
