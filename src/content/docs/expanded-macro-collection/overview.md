Expanded Macro Collection is an Atlassian Forge app for Confluence Cloud that bundles 13 practical macros behind one app and one Marketplace listing. It spans four areas teams ask for most: governance and compliance, navigation and discovery, admin reporting, and everyday authoring. Every macro is built on Atlassian's UI Kit, so the app is eligible for Runs on Atlassian: your data never leaves your Atlassian Cloud environment.

## Why Expanded Macro Collection?

| Challenge | How the collection helps |
| --- | --- |
| "Prove that staff have read this policy." | The Sign-off and Acknowledgement macro records who acknowledged each page, at what version and when, with an editor-only roster and a space-wide audit CSV. |
| "Mark and track sensitive pages." | The Page Classification Banner labels a page (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted), shows a banner, stamps the marking into PDF and Word exports, and rolls up every classified page for a space. |
| "What links to this page, and what else is related?" | Referenced By and Related Pages answer both from a self-maintained, permission-aware link index that Confluence's own search cannot provide. |
| "Where does our documentation stand, and what is stale?" | The Status Rollup and Stale Content reports turn per-page signals into space-wide dashboards for content audits. |
| "Make long documents and exports look professional." | Numbered Headings, the PDF Page Divider, Page Status, Review Reminder, the Page Metadata Card, and Advanced Children Display cover day-to-day authoring. |

| Role | Use case |

## Key features

**Governance and compliance**

**Sign-off and Acknowledgement** with a per-version roster and an audit CSV.

**Page Classification Banner** with export marking and a space-wide rollup.

**Content Visibility** to restrict who sees a block of content.

**Navigation and discovery**

**Referenced By** backlinks and **Related Pages** by shared links, both permission-aware.

**Numbered Headings** that stay numbered on view and in export.

**Admin reporting**

**Status Rollup Report** and **Stale Content Report** dashboards.

**Advanced Children Display** and the **Page Metadata Card**.

**Authoring**

**PDF Page Divider**, **Page Status Badge**, and **Review Reminder**.

## How it works

Install the app on your Confluence site, then add any macro to a page from the macro browser. Per-space settings (workflow statuses, classifications, and the acknowledgement audit) live under Space Settings, then Integrations, then Expanded Macro Collection. Referenced By and Related Pages build a link index automatically in the background and keep it current as pages change. No custom fields, no external services, and no admin scripting are required.

## Runs on Atlassian

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge infrastructure and makes no outbound network calls. It reads Confluence with your own permissions, so you only ever see content you are allowed to see.
