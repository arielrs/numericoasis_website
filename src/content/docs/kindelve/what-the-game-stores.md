This page is a plain summary of what the game stores. The binding version is the
[privacy policy](https://play.kindelve.com/privacy/), with the
[terms of use](https://play.kindelve.com/terms/), both in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and
Japanese. If this page and the policy ever disagree, the policy is right.

## On your device

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your save | The game. Creatures, mutations, relics, echoes, records, deeds, the ledger |
| A delve in progress | So a reload or a put-down phone does not lose the delve |
| Your settings | Language, the look, the lettering size, sound, the menu |
| A random device id and its account's id | A string like `dev-k3f9qa`, made by the game, and the account the server made for it |

All of it is in your browser's own storage for play.kindelve.com. One cookie, `kd_session`, signs
the device in to its account; scripts on the page cannot read it, and it only goes to
play.kindelve.com. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

## On the server

Every player has an account from their first visit, a guest with no sign-up, known to the server by
random numbers only. The server keeps:

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| The account, and a copy of your save, so it is recoverable and follows you between devices | Until you delete it, or 24 months after you last played |
| A log of save changes, used to keep the Trial board fair | 30 days |
| Your Warden's name, if you choose one | Until you take it off the board or delete the account |
| Trial entries: the device id, the name shown, the delve's inputs and its result | 90 days |
| The playing-now count: the device id and when it last said the game was open | One day |
| Your email address or Google account, if you sign in | Until you delete the account |
| Access records: IP address, date and time, which Brazil's Marco Civil da Internet requires | Six months |

An account with an email address is warned by email 30 days before it would be deleted for being
idle; opening the game keeps it.

## Your data, in the game

In Options, Your Warden:

- **Download my data**: a file with everything the server keeps about your account.
- **Delete my account**: all of it at once, except the access records the law requires us to keep
  for six months.
- **Take my name off the board**, so the board goes back to a name of its own.

## What is not collected

No real name, no password, no advertising identifier, because there is no advertising. No
analytics in the game, no third-party tracking scripts, no camera, no microphone, no location, no
contacts. The one outside script is Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person is asking for
a sign-in link, and it loads only on the email sign-in form.
