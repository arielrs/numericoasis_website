A delve is the game's only timeline. Everything else is preparation for one, or a consequence of
one.

## Floors, strata and sight

The deep is numbered in **floors** from one, with no bottom that anyone has found. Floors are
grouped into **strata**, and a stratum is the unit that changes the rules: the creatures that stand
in it, and the law that holds there.

You can see six floors at a time. What lies below that is known only by what the Codex remembers of
it.

## Time

The simulation runs at twenty ticks a second, and it is the same twenty ticks on every device. That
matters more than it sounds. It is why a delve played on a phone and the same delve played on a
desktop produce an identical result, and it is what lets the server check a Trial score by running
it again.

## Keepers

Every tenth floor holds a **Keeper**: one creature, far stronger than the floor it stands on, with a
clock. Keepers are the game's pacing. The floors between them are where a build comes together, and
the Keeper is the question of whether it came together enough.

A Keeper can carry a **find**: an egg, a relic, something the ordinary floors do not give.

## Tries

A **try** is spent when your Kin falls. The delve continues. When the tries run out, the delve ends
and banks.

The deepest floor reached before the first try was spent is recorded separately, as a **flawless**
depth. Some deeds ask for it, and it is a more honest measure of a build than the floor you
eventually stopped on.

## Ichor and upgrades

**Ichor** is earned by killing and spent inside the delve on ranks in named traits. Costs grow as
ranks stack, so the question is never whether you can afford one but whether this is the trait worth
pouring the next thousand into.

Ichor does not survive the delve.

## Caches

A **cache** stands on some floors and offers two things, one on the left and one on the right. You
pick one, and the window closes on its own if you do not. A standing choice can be set once, so the
game takes the same side for that kind of cache every time without asking.

## The laws of the deep

Each stratum holds a **law**, which changes the rules rather than the numbers:

- Floors where the dead leave piles that help whoever stands on them next.
- Floors that vent, warning first, then hurting everything still caught in them.
- Floors where something is written, and reading it finds a page of the story.
- Floors that cut a leg off the route, shortening the walk.

A law is not a difficulty setting. It is the reason this stratum is not the last one.

## The week's law

One law holds across the whole deep for a week, announced on the Descend band, the same for every
player. It is the game's weekly shape: a week where ghosts give more ichor and a week where bones
help more are different weeks, and the deed attached to the week's law can only be paid that week.
