This page is the shortest path from opening the game to understanding what you are doing.

## 1. Choose a Kin

You begin with three creatures, one from each starting family. They are not equivalent: one is
sturdy, one is quick, one hits harder than it can take. Any of them reaches the first Keeper.

## 2. Send it down

Press Descend. The creature walks down from the floor you chose and fights whatever stands on each
floor, between one and five creatures at a time. You are not controlling the fight. You are
watching it and deciding what to do with what it earns.

## 3. Spend the ichor while it falls

Ichor accumulates as the creature kills. It buys **upgrades**: ranks in named traits, offered a few
at a time, each making the creature stronger for the rest of this delve only.

The first instinct is to save ichor. Resist it. Ichor is worth nothing once the delve ends, so the
only wrong amount to spend is less than all of it. There is a button that spends it all for you,
and later a habit that keeps doing so without being asked.

## 4. Use the four skills

Your Kin takes four **skills** down with it, shown as buttons. These are the only
moment-to-moment decisions in a delve: a burst of damage, a shield, a heal. They come back on a
timer.

If a Kin knows more than four, the game asks which four to take before you descend, and remembers
the answer for next time.

## 5. Meet a Keeper, and stop

On floor ten stands the first Keeper. It is several times what the floors before it were. One of
two things happens.

- **It falls.** Keep going. Floor twenty has another one, and it is harder.
- **Your Kin falls.** You spend a **try**. Tries are limited, and when they run out the delve ends
  and banks what it earned.

Ending a delve on purpose, before the tries run out, is a real choice and often the right one. The
deepest floor you reached is what the game records, and a delve you end yourself banks exactly as
much as one that ended badly.

## 6. Spend the echoes

Back on the surface, open the Warden tab. Echoes buy ranks in Warden skills, which apply to every
delve from then on. This is where progress actually lives: a delve makes one creature stronger for
five minutes, and a Warden skill makes every creature stronger for good.

## What to do next

Send the same Kin down again. The second delve starts from the strength the first one paid for, and
it will stop deeper. Once that stops being true, the rest of the game is the answer to why:
[Kin](/documentation/kindelve/kin/) for who you send,
[Builds](/documentation/kindelve/builds/) for what they become on the way down, and
[The Warden](/documentation/kindelve/warden/) for what you are becoming.
