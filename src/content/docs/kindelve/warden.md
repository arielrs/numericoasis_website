A delve makes one creature stronger for five minutes. The Warden tab is where you become stronger
for good.

## Warden skills

**Echoes** are banked by ending a delve, and they buy ranks in Warden skills. These apply to every
delve from then on, across every Kin.

The tree is long and it is not a straight line: some branches make the early floors quick, some
make the deep floors survivable, and some pay for themselves only if you play a particular way.
Nothing in it is a trap, and nothing in it is mandatory.

## Deeds and Milestones

**Deeds** are the game's list of things worth doing: reach a floor, fell a Keeper flawlessly, hold
a synergy to the end. Many unlock a keystone, which is why the keystone list grows as you play
rather than arriving complete.

**Milestones** track the same idea per Kin line and per account, with stars for the ones that took
something. Some deeds carry a gift: an egg, echoes, a provision.

## Paths

The rift is the first delve, not the only one. **Paths** are found, and each has its own record and
its own chosen start floor. A path is a different delve rather than a longer one, and the Descend
tab is where you choose which.

## Vows

A **vow** is a condition you take on before descending, with ranks, and a bounty for the line that
carries it. Vows are the game's difficulty dial in the honest direction: you choose to make a delve
harder, and the delve pays for it.

## Forays

A Kin can be sent on a **foray** instead of a delve, for one, four or eight hours. It comes home
with echoes, kills and sometimes a mutation. A Kin on a foray is not available to descend, which is
the whole cost.

Forays are what the game does while you are not playing it. They are deliberately not the main
engine: the game is not trying to be something you check on.

## Provisions

**Provisions** are bought on the surface and packed before a delve, and they are used up when it
starts. They are a way to spend a surplus on a specific attempt, which is usually a Keeper you have
failed twice.

## Collapses and Sigils

A **Collapse** is the game's reset: you fold everything up and begin again, and you keep **Sigils**
to buy rules that change how the next run works. It is the layer underneath all the others, and it
is not something a first week needs to think about.
