**Kin** are the creatures you send into the deep. The roster is the long game: a delve is five
minutes, and a Kin line is something you build over weeks.

## Lines and forms

A Kin is not one creature, it is a **line**. Every line has three forms, and a line that the deep
has rewritten enough times becomes its next form permanently, on the surface, before any further
delve. It stays that form in the Kin tab, in the Compendium and on every descent after.

Evolution used to be bought with ichor in the middle of a delve and lost when it ended. It is
permanent now, and it happens where you can see it.

## Families

Lines belong to families, and a family shares a skill. Families are the first thing that makes a
roster feel like a choice rather than a collection: sending down a second member of a family you
have already invested in is cheaper than starting again elsewhere.

## Mutations

**Mutations** attach to a line, not to your account. Two players with the same starting creature
will not have the same creature a month later, because the deep rewrote theirs differently.

Mutations are also the line's progress bar. How many a line holds is what gates its next form, so
a line you keep sending down is a line that is becoming something.

## Strains

A line can hold **strains**, and one strain can be picked as the form it goes down as. A strain is
a variant of the same line rather than a different creature: the same silhouette, a different
proposition.

## Relics

Where mutations belong to a line, **relics** belong to the account, and each copy stacks up to a
cap. The cap is deliberate: it is what guarantees a ceiling exists, so time spent buys reaching it
sooner rather than passing it.

## Eggs and the Hen-Wife's Choice

Keepers carry **eggs**, on a clock rather than purely on luck, so a long run of nothing does not
happen. An egg hatches into a new line.

Once The Hen-Wife's Choice is known, an egg offers up to three lines and you keep one. A choice is
better than a gift: it is the difference between the deep giving you something and the deep asking
what you need.

## Gilded creatures

Some creatures in the deep are **gilded**. They are rarer, they are worth more ichor, and the Codex
counts them separately. A gilded Keeper is a small event.

## The Codex

The **Codex** remembers everything the deep has shown you: around nine hundred creatures, each with
a page that opens further the more of them you fell. It holds the Compendium of your own Kin, the
Bestiary of what stands against them, the pages of the story found on written floors, the Warden's
notes, and the record of every build you have taken down.

The Codex is where the game's reading lives. None of it is required to play, and all of it is the
reason the deep feels like a place rather than a number going up.
