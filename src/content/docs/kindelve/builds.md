A build is what a Kin becomes during one delve. It is assembled from three things, in this order:
the upgrades you buy, the keystone you take, and the synergies you hold.

## Upgrades

**Upgrades** are ranks in named traits, bought with ichor from a few offers at a time. Ranks cost
more as they stack.

Three conveniences exist once bought, and they matter more than they sound:

- **Ten, a hundred or the most ranks at once**, so a late delve is not a thousand taps.
- **Spend it all**, which buys the cheapest of the offered upgrades repeatedly while the ichor
  lasts.
- **A Standing Order**, a habit that keeps spending without being asked for the rest of the delve.

An upgrade new to this line is marked as new, and the game says why it is being offered.

## Keystones

At three points in a delve, the deep offers **keystones**: three of them, with a draw again if you
would rather see others. Take one, or take none, and the offer passes on its own after ten seconds
if you do not answer.

There are twenty-two, and they are builds rather than bonuses. A keystone changes what your Kin is
doing, not how much of it: one turns thorns into the point of a shield, one makes a crit the thing
you aim the whole delve at, one makes speed the resource. Most are unlocked by deeds rather than
being available from the start.

Each keystone carries a **rank** badge on the upgrade cards it affects, so you can see what the
keystone has made important without reading its text again.

## Synergies

**Synergies** are twelve pairings between traits. When a pairing is ready, it can be held in one of
two chosen places, and a held synergy climbs **tiers** the longer it stays held to the end of a
delve.

Two chosen places, not twelve, is what makes a synergy a decision. The question is never whether a
synergy is good, it is which two are worth the places.

## Mastery and resonance

The game counts how many delves you have taken each keystone down, and the deepest floor you
reached with it. That is **keystone mastery**, and it is visible in the Codex under Builds.

Mastery is not a power curve in disguise. It is a record, so that "this is my build" becomes a
thing the game knows about you rather than a thing you tell yourself.

## Reading a build after the fact

Every delve lands in the **ledger**, with the keystone taken, the synergies held and the tier each
reached. The ledger is the honest answer to why a delve went deeper than the last one, and it is
usually not the reason you assumed while you were playing it.
