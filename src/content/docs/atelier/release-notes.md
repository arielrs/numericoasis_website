## Version 2.3

**Release Date:** April 15, 2026

Initial public release of Atelier for Confluence Cloud.

### Design Macros

- **Background:** page sections with a pattern or colour background, rich text, and an optional embedded page.
- **Banner:** decorative headers with centred text and pattern fills.
- **Text Color:** custom colours for any block of text.
- **Divider:** styled separators: solid, dashed, dotted, gradient, or pattern.
- **Info Panel:** information boxes with 12 icons, 7 border styles, and accent colours.
- **Button:** call-to-action links in 4 shapes (Rounded, Soft, Pill, and Square), with emoji and pattern fills.
- **Spacer:** adjustable whitespace with an optional background.
- **Card:** content cards with patterned headers and call-to-action links.

### Patterns and Editing

- **64 background patterns** in four categories (Gradients, Geometric, Organic, and Technical), drawn without external images and coloured with up to three of your colours.
- **Live preview** in each macro's configuration panel.
- **Rich text editor** with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, headings, lists, links, emoji, highlighting, and font sizes.
- **Colour picker** with hex codes, opacity, and the space's palette.

### Space Settings

- **Colour palettes:** up to 50 brand colours per space.
- **Theme presets:** up to 50 saved combinations.
- **Suggested fonts:** up to 30 fonts.

### Export and Access

- Styling carries into PDF and Word exports.
- Atelier macros also render for anonymous and unlicensed viewers of a page.
- Runs on Atlassian Forge with no external servers, and deletes its stored data when the app is uninstalled.
