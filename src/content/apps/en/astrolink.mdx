faq:
  - q: "How do I see a dependency map in Jira?"
    a: "Install Astrolink and open any work item: its links, parents, subtasks and linked Confluence pages appear as a graph in the issue panel. To map more than one work item, open Astrolink from the Jira app launcher and start from a JQL query, a saved filter or a space. Without an app, Jira Premium and Enterprise include a dependencies view in Plans, and each work item lists its own linked work items."
  - q: "Can Astrolink show dependencies across Jira spaces?"
    a: "Yes. Astrolink follows work item links into any space, and clicking a node takes you to that work item wherever it lives. Color the nodes or cluster them by space to see which dependencies leave your team. Linked work items in spaces you cannot open appear as locked nodes with no details shown."
  - q: "Will people see work items they do not have permission to view?"
    a: "No. Every Jira request Astrolink makes runs as the person viewing the map, with their own permissions. A linked work item they cannot open appears as a locked node with no metadata, so the shape of the graph stays visible without exposing a summary, a status or an assignee. Astrolink has read-only access and cannot change any work item."
  - q: "What data does Astrolink read and store?"
    a: "It reads work item details such as key, summary, type, status, assignee and links to draw the map, and holds them in memory for that request. It saves your presets, saved maps and locked layouts in Atlassian's Forge storage, sandboxed to your site, and display preferences in your browser. Locked layouts expire after 30 days, and uninstalling deletes everything the app stored."
  - q: "Is Astrolink secure, and does data leave Atlassian?"
    a: "Astrolink runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, and no data leaves your Atlassian site. It makes no external network calls, uses the read:jira-work, read:jira-user and storage:app scopes, and never logs account IDs or work item keys. The Marketplace listing shows the Runs on Atlassian badge and the completed privacy and security questionnaire for your review."
  - q: "Does Astrolink work with Jira Data Center?"
    a: "No. Astrolink is a Jira Cloud app built on Forge, with no Data Center or Server version. It works in software, service and business spaces, and with Jira Product Discovery: ideas linked to delivery work appear in the graph, and you see an idea's own map by opening it in the standard Jira issue view."
  - q: "How long does setup take, and who can install Astrolink?"
    a: "Setup is one install and one setting per space. A Jira site or organization admin installs Astrolink from the Atlassian Marketplace, and other users can request it from the listing. A Jira admin then opens a work item, adds Astrolink from the View App Actions icon in the issue panel area and chooses Show for all work items, and the map appears on every work item in that space."
  - q: "How much does Astrolink cost?"
    a: "Astrolink is a paid app sold through the Atlassian Marketplace, with a free trial so you can map your own dependencies before you buy. Current pricing is on the Marketplace listing, which is where it stays accurate, and you pay through your Atlassian account like any other Marketplace app."
