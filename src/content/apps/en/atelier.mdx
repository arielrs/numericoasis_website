faq:
  - q: "How do I add a background color to a section of a Confluence page?"
    a: "Wrap the section in Atelier's Background macro, choose one of 64 patterns and up to three colors, and write the content inside it with headings, lists, links and emoji. Without an app, the closest built-in option is a panel in one of 20 background colors. Company Hub sections also take a custom background color, but sections are available only in Company Hub, not on regular pages."
  - q: "How do I add a banner to a Confluence page?"
    a: "Insert Atelier's Banner macro for a decorative header with centered text and a pattern fill in your brand colors. Confluence also has two built-in options: a header image above the page title, from Unsplash or your own upload, and on Premium and Enterprise plans, a Spotlight block with a large image, a colored background and a button. Atelier's Card and Button macros add links below a banner."
  - q: "Can I use custom hex colors in Confluence Cloud?"
    a: "Yes, with Atelier: the Text Color macro accepts any hex code, and a space admin can save up to 50 named brand colors, with hex codes and opacity, for every Atelier macro to offer. In the built-in editor, text and panels take their colors from Confluence's own palettes, while the Spotlight block on Premium and Enterprise accepts custom HEX, RGB or HSL values."
  - q: "What data does Atelier store, and is it secure?"
    a: "Atelier stores each space's design settings, meaning brand colors, theme presets and suggested fonts, and nothing about the content of your pages. The app is built on Atlassian Forge and runs inside Atlassian with no external servers, so the settings stay in encrypted Forge storage. Uninstalling removes them automatically, and Atelier holds no personal data."
  - q: "Can a reader see an embedded page they do not have permission to open?"
    a: "No. Page embedding in the Background macro follows each reader's own Confluence permissions, so the embedded content appears only for people who can open the source page. Page restrictions and space permissions apply exactly as they do elsewhere in Confluence, and Atelier does not widen anyone's access."
  - q: "Does Atelier work on Confluence Data Center?"
    a: "No. Atelier is a Confluence Cloud app built on Atlassian Forge, and its Marketplace listing covers Cloud only. Teams moving from Data Center can rebuild their styled pages with its macros once they are on Cloud, using brand colors, theme presets and fonts set in each space's settings."
  - q: "How much does Atelier cost, and is there a free trial?"
    a: "Pricing is on the Atlassian Marketplace listing, and you can start a free trial there before you buy. Atelier is a paid app billed through Atlassian alongside your other Marketplace apps. Use the trial to design one real page, such as a space homepage or a team news page, before you decide."
  - q: "Who can install Atelier, and how long does setup take?"
    a: "A site or organization admin installs Atelier from the Atlassian Marketplace, and anyone else can request it from the listing. Authors can add the macros to pages as soon as it is installed. Brand colors, theme presets and suggested fonts are set later by a space admin in space settings, once per space, whenever that space wants a shared look."
